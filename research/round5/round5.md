# Round 5: can MVSep's Mega 53 (MIT weights) replace the Demucs models for all six stems?

Date: 2026-11-24. htdemucs and htdemucs_6s weights are "for scientific purposes only" (round 1). Mega 53's are MIT. This round scores Mega 53's heads on every stem Fretboard's mixer plays (vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano/keys, other) against today's Demucs-based recipes, on the 26 Cambridge-MT remixes of round 4, and measures what each recipe costs on the Mac.

Full tables (pooled, per set, per item, every head choice): [tables.md](tables.md), generated by `r5_summary.py` from [scores/](scores/) (one JSON per item).

HEADLINE

## Data

The 26 Cambridge-MT remixes of round 4 ([../round4/round4.md](../round4/round4.md), `r4_mixcmt.py`): `cmt8` (8 MUSDB test songs, 90 s each) and `cmtx` (18 non-MUSDB rock excerpts, 18–39 s), 20.2 min in all. Each item stores mixture, vocals, drums, bass, guitar and, where the song has them, keys and other. Every mixture is a plain sum of its parts:

- **"other" is stored only when the song has such parts** (9 of 26 items; banjo, mandolin, strings, synth pads, FX and the like). Where it is missing it is silence. I score other against mix − vocals − drums − bass − guitar − keys; that equals `other.wav` (or silence) within **−145 dB** of the mix on every item (`parts_sum_err_db` in each score JSON).
- **keys** exist on 10 of 26 items (cmt8: Angels In Amplifiers, Ben Carrigan, Al James, Signe Jakobsen; cmtx: Avalon, Impossible Colours, JetB, Man About A Dog, Peter White, StreetNoise). The recipes' "piano" stem is scored against this keys group (piano, organ, synths, e-pianos by file name).
- A stem's **uSDR** is only defined on items that have it. "Stem removed" uSDR is defined on all 26.

## Recipes

| name | vocals | drums / bass | guitar | piano (keys) | other | how it ran |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hyb6` | mean of htdemucs and htdemucs_6s | mean of both | 6s | 6s | mean 4-stem other − guitar − piano (README `-n ensemble`, 6-stem view) | `htdemucs-rs separate -n ensemble`, shifts 0 (one pass), float32 WAV, no clipping |

| `mega_mix` | Mega `vocal` head on the mix | Mega `drums` / `bass` | Mega `guitar` | Mega `piano` | **mix − the five** (Mega has no "other" head) | `r5_mega.py`, PyTorch 2.6.0 CPU fp32, 8 threads |
| `kim_mega` | Kim (the chain's vocals) | Mega heads on Kim's instrumental | Mega `guitar` on the instrumental | Mega `piano` on the instrumental | mix − the five | as above, input = mix − Kim vocals |

Head choices tried (all reported in tables.md; vocal choice only on the mix):
- vocals: `vocal` head, or `lead-vocal` + `back-vocal` summed (`_lb`);
- piano stem: `piano` (default), `keys` (`_keys`), `piano` + `keys` (`_pk`), and all five keyboard heads `piano` + `keys` + `digital-piano` + `organ` + `synth` (`_kb5`).

Also scored, for context:
- `hyb6_fix`, `chain_fix`: the Demucs recipes with other := mix − the five, i.e. the same "residual other" construction as the Mega recipes (and the construction today's mixer uses for its "other").
- `chain+mg`: the chain with Mega's guitar (on Kim's instrumental), other := residual; `chain+mgk`: also Mega's piano. Round 4's recommendation was the first.

**Mega inference** is round 4's (`r4_mega.py`: MSST's generic `demix`, 20 s chunks, overlap 2, fp32), in a copy (`r5_mega.py`) that builds only the requested heads and memory-maps the checkpoint (peak RSS 4.2 GB at 8 threads with 11 heads, against ≥ 7.8 GB for round 4's loader, which built all 53 heads first). It ran on the CPU: on this Mac (M1 Pro, 16 GB), MPS took 85 s per 20 s chunk and 6.6 GB of GPU memory, the CPU 28 s.
**Checks:** Mega's guitar on the Mac CPU equals round 4's CUDA run to 0.001 dB uSDR (Avalon: 7.326 / 7.553 dB on mix / instrumental in both rounds); CPU vs MPS outputs agree to 110–119 dB; the chain's guitar (Rust, f16 Kim weights) equals round 4's PyTorch `6s_inst` (4.372 dB).

## Metrics (`r5_score.py`)

As round 4, per stem:
- **uSDR** = 10·log10(Σs² / Σ(s − ŝ)²) over the item, both channels.
- **stem removed** = what the mixer's mute plays, mix − ŝ, against the truth mix − s: 10·log10(Σ(mix − s)² / Σ(s − ŝ)²). Within an item it ranks variants exactly as uSDR does; it is also defined on items without the part (there it measures how loudly a stem that should be silent plays).
- **level on items without the part**: 10·log10(Σŝ² / Σmix²).
- **sum-to-mix residual** per recipe: 10·log10(Σ(mix − Σ stems)² / Σmix²).
- **Statistics:** paired per item against `hyb6`; brackets are 95 % bootstrap intervals over items (2000 resamples, seed 0); k/n = items improved.

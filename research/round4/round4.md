# Round 4: measuring the guitar stem (exact ground truth), htdemucs_6s vs MVSep Mega 53

Date: 2026-11-24. This round scores the stem Fretboard's mixer mutes or solos: guitar. MUSDB has no guitar ground truth, so I built five sets where the guitar is known exactly. I then scored htdemucs_6s and the MVSep Mega 53-stem BS-RoFormer on them, on the mix and on Kim's instrumental.

Full tables (per set, pooled, per item): [tables.md](tables.md), generated by `r4_summary.py` from [scores/](scores/) (one JSON per item).

## Headline

1. **Mega 53's guitar head beats htdemucs_6s's guitar on every one of 50 items.**
   - On the 26 real multitrack songs (Cambridge-MT), guitar uSDR is **6.92 dB vs 4.28** (6s on the mix): **+2.64 [+2.02, +3.25], 26/26**.
   - Against round 3's chain guitar (6s on Kim's instrumental) it is **+2.16 [+1.62, +2.75], 26/26**.
   - cSDR agrees: 6.92 vs 4.22.
2. **"Guitar removed" (what the mixer's mute plays) improves by the same amount.** It is 13.85 dB vs 11.22 for 6s on the mix and 11.69 for the chain.
3. **Mega's guitar carries much less of the other instruments.**
   - Keys in the guitar stem: −22.9 dB vs −12.1 dB for 6s (relative to the stem's energy). 6s puts piano/organ into "guitar".
   - Vocals: −27.6 vs −21.9 dB. Drums: −24.2 vs −19.9 dB.
4. **Running on Kim's instrumental helps both models, but helps Mega much less.**
   - 6s: +0.48 dB (24/26).
   - Mega: +0.16 [+0.08, +0.26] (22/26).
5. **Rejected:**
   - shifts 2 on 6s: +0.03 dB for twice the CPU;
   - averaging 6s and Mega guitars: −0.60 vs Mega alone, 22/26 worse;
   - Mega's electric-guitar + acoustic-guitar heads summed instead of its "guitar" head: −0.06, 22/26 worse.
6. **Cost.** On the box's CPU (i5-12450H, 1 thread):
   - Mega with only the guitar head costs **11.4 CPU-s per audio-s**, against 0.92 for an htdemucs_6s pass and 14.3 for Kim (30 s excerpt).
   - With all 53 heads it costs 16.0.
   - Its checkpoint is fp16: 682 M parameters = 26.3 M backbone + 53 heads × 12.4 M. **A guitar-only model is 38.7 M parameters, 77 MB in fp16.**

## Data: five sets with exact guitar ground truth

Every mixture is a plain sum of its parts at fixed gains, with no effects. So the guitar reference, and the "no guitar" mix (mixture − guitar), are exact. Parts sum to the mixture within −145 dB. Licences are in [../datasets.md](../datasets.md).

| set | what | items | audio | guitar re mix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cmt8` | Cambridge-MT **Full multitracks of 8 of our 12 MUSDB test songs**, remixed by `r4_mixcmt.py`, 60–150 s of each song | 8 | 12.0 min | −6.0 to −8.7 dB |
| `cmtx` | Cambridge-MT Edited Excerpts of **18 rock / alt-rock projects not in MUSDB18** (by song or artist) | 18 | 8.2 min | −7.1 to −9.2 dB |
| `gtechs` | Guitar-TECHS P3 electric (amp-miked) over a MUSDB test song's vocals + drums + bass | 8 × 30 s | 3.9 min | −3.7 to −5.1 dB |
| `egdb` | EGDB-PG electric (DI through BIAS FX2 amp presets) over MUSDB vocals + drums + bass | 8 × 30 s | 4.0 min | −3.6 to −15.7 dB |
| `gset` | GuitarSet acoustic (mic; comp then solo) over MUSDB vocals + drums + bass | 8 × 30 s | 4.0 min | −3.2 to −4.8 dB |

### How the Cambridge-MT remix is made (`r4_mixcmt.py`; every track's group, gain and pan is in [cmt_manifest.json](cmt_manifest.json))

- **Grouping.** Each raw track goes into vocals, drums (incl. percussion), bass, guitar, keys or other, by file name. This follows MUSDB's grouping, with "other" split into guitar, keys and the rest.
  - Scratch, guide and reference tracks are left out.
  - A guitar track named DI is left out when the same part has an amp/mic track. A DI isn't what a mix uses.
- **Pan.** Mono guitar parts alternate left/right at ±0.6, constant power; two mics of one part share a side. Everything else is centred, and stereo files are kept as they are.
- **Gains.**
  - Within a group, tracks sum at unity (as recorded).
  - Each group is then set to a fixed level relative to the vocals, measured as power over the seconds where that group plays: drums 0, bass −3, guitar −3, keys −7, other −7 dB.
  - The vocals / drums / bass / other levels are the medians of MUSDB's own stems on our 12 test songs.
  - The mix is then scaled to a 0.9 peak.
- **What's in the guitar group.**
  - Electric and acoustic guitars, including double-tracks.
  - Signe Jakobsen's MIDI-triggered electric guitars count as guitar. Al James's two MIDI guitars were left out by a naming bug, fixed before Signe was built; that remix is still exact, just without those two parts.
  - Banjo, mandolin and dulcimer count as "other".

### Open sets (`r4_mixopen.py`)

- **Construction.**
  - A guitar recording (mono, centred) is set to 3 dB below the backing's RMS.
  - The backing is a MUSDB test track's vocals + drums + bass, starting 30 s in. The track's own "other", where its guitars are, is left out.
  - Then the first 30 s are kept.
- **Limits.**
  - Guitar and backing share neither key nor tempo, and there are no keys in the backing.
  - Use these sets only to rank models against each other.

## Variants

All passes ran on the Linux box's RTX 4060 in fp32, under the shared GPU lock: `r4_sep.py`, `r4_kim_sep.py` and `r4_mega.py`, driven by `r4_kim.sh`, `r4_queue6s.sh` and `r4_queue_mega.sh`.

| name | what |
|---|---|
| `6s_s0` | htdemucs_6s guitar on the mix, shifts 0 (PyTorch Demucs 4.0.1, overlap 0.25) |
| `6s_s2` | the same at shifts 2 (`random.seed(0)`); today's mixer runs 6s at shifts 2 |
| `6s_inst` | 6s on Kim's instrumental (mix − Kim Mel-RoFormer vocals): round 3's chain |
| `mega_mix` | Mega 53's `guitar` head on the mix |
| `mega_inst` | Mega 53's `guitar` head on Kim's instrumental |
| `megaEA_*` | Mega 53's `electric-guitar` + `acoustic-guitar` heads summed |
| `avg(a,b)` | the mean of two guitar estimates |

**Mega 53 inference.**
- MSST's `demix` in generic mode: 20 s chunks, overlap 2, linear fades of chunk/10, reflect padding.
- Only the needed mask heads are kept. The backbone is shared, so the other heads don't change these outputs.
- fp32 without autocast; it fits in the 8 GB card.
- The MPS and CPU outputs agree with each other to about 40 dB.

**Kim on CUDA** ran with the repo's `torch.cuda.amp.autocast` disabled, so it computes in fp32 like rounds 2–3's CPU/MPS runs.

## Metrics (`r4_score.py`)

- **guitar uSDR:** 10·log10(Σg² / Σ(g − ĝ)²) over the item, both channels.
- **guitar removed uSDR:** the mixer's mute plays mix − ĝ; the truth is mix − g. So the error is the same g − ĝ, measured against the energy of everything else: 10·log10(Σ(mix − g)² / Σ(g − ĝ)²).
  - It ranks variants exactly like guitar uSDR within an item.
  - Its level says how loud the leftover guitar and the holes are relative to the remaining music.
- **cSDR:** museval BSSEval v4, median over 1 s windows. It uses two sources, guitar and the rest (mix − g), estimated by ĝ and mix − ĝ; two sources keep museval affordable. "removed cSDR" is the second source's.
- **bleed:** per 1 s window and channel, ĝ is least-squares fitted on all true parts (guitar, vocals, drums, bass, keys, other).
  - Each part's fitted share is summed over windows, in dB relative to the estimate's energy. Lower is less bleed.
  - "unexplained" is what no part explains: artefacts and distortion.
  - Correlated parts can trade share, so read it as a ranking, not a decomposition.
- **Statistics:** paired per item. Brackets are 95 % bootstrap intervals over items; "k/n" is how many items improved.

### Bleed: what's in each guitar stem (Cambridge-MT, dB re the stem's energy)

- **Keys are 6s's main leak.**
  - Its guitar stem holds piano/organ at −12.1 dB, about 10 dB more than Mega's −22.9. Kim doesn't change that.
  - On songs with keys (Ben Carrigan, Signe Jakobsen, StreetNoise, Impossible Colours), muting 6s's guitar also takes out keys.
- **Vocals.** Kim's instrumental cuts vocal bleed in 6s's guitar by 4.3 dB (−21.9 → −26.2), round 3's proxy said 2.1. In Mega's it falls only 1.3 dB, because Mega already keeps vocals out.
- **Artefacts dominate everyone's error.**
  - "unexplained" is −6 to −8 dB: missing or distorted guitar, not other instruments.
  - That's why the absolute scores on dense rock (4–7 dB) are low for both models.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| htdemucs_6s, 1 pass | **0.92** | shifts 2 = 2 passes | 0.08 | 55 MB fp16 |

Round 3 described the checkpoint as "1.37 GB fp32, ≈342 M parameters". It is fp16 with **682 M** parameters, almost all in the 53 mask heads. A guitar-only Mega is small: 77 MB is about Demucs' size. It is not cheap to run: about 12× a Demucs pass on this CPU.

## Recommendation for the mixer's guitar stem (quality, then CPU, then size)

Δ is guitar uSDR vs today's 6s on the mix, pooled over the 26 Cambridge-MT songs. "Extra CPU" is on top of round 3's recommended recipe (Kim → htdemucs + 6s on the instrumental), which already pays for Kim. Box CPU, 1 thread.

| rank | guitar stem | guitar uSDR | Δ vs today | guitar removed | keys / vocals bleed | extra CPU-s per audio-s | extra weights |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Mega 53 guitar head on Kim's instrumental** (`mega_inst`) | **6.92** | **+2.64 (26/26)** | **13.85** | −22.9 / −27.6 | **+11.4** | +77 MB |
| 2 | Mega 53 guitar head on the mix (`mega_mix`); needs no Kim | 6.76 | +2.48 (26/26) | 13.69 | −22.8 / −26.3 | +11.4, or 11.4 alone without Kim | +77 MB |
| 3 | 6s on Kim's instrumental (`6s_inst`, round 3's pick) | 4.76 | +0.48 (24/26) | 11.69 | −12.8 / −26.2 | 0 (already in the recipe) | 0 |
| 4 | 6s on the mix, shifts 0 (`6s_s0`) | 4.28 | – | 11.22 | −12.1 / −21.9 | – | – |
| – | today: 6s on the mix, shifts 2 (`6s_s2`) | 4.32 | +0.03 | 11.25 | −12.1 / −21.8 | 2 × 0.92 | – |

- **Pick rank 1** if the guitar stem matters more than CPU, and for Fretboard it does.
  - It's the largest quality gain any round has found for the stem the product is built around: +2.6 dB on real songs, every one of 26.
  - Muting guitar then leaves a cleaner song, and the stem no longer takes keys with it.
  - Cost: Kim (14.3) + htdemucs + 6s (about 1.8) + Mega guitar (11.4) is about 27.5 CPU-s per audio-s on this CPU.
  - On the GPU it's about 1.1 s per audio-s in all.
- **Keep 6s in the recipe for piano and for the mixer's "other"**, and take only guitar from Mega.
  - Mega's piano/keys heads weren't scored here.
  - Today the mixer builds other = mix − vocals − drums − bass − guitar, so a cleaner guitar also gives a cleaner "other".
- **Rank 2** is the choice if Kim is ever dropped. It is only 0.16 dB below rank 1 (22/26 worse).
- **Don't**:
  - average 6s and Mega (−0.6);
  - sum Mega's electric + acoustic heads in place of its "guitar" head (−0.06);
  - run 6s at shifts 2 (+0.03 for 2× CPU).
- **Rust port:** Mega is a BS-RoFormer. Its backbone (dim 256, depth 12, 62 bands, 20 s chunks) is close to the Mel-RoFormer port already under way for Kim. A guitar-only export needs the backbone plus one head.

## Caveats

- **Mega 53's training data is undisclosed.**
  - ZFTurbo says he doesn't hold the copyright to all of it.
  - MVSep's models are commonly trained on large multitrack collections. Cambridge-MT, MUSDB, Guitar-TECHS (published 2025, before Mega's April 2026 release), EGDB and GuitarSet may all be in it.
  - The **+12.5 dB on Guitar-TECHS** is far above every other set (+2.2 to +4.8). On several of its items 6s scores below 3 dB and Mega above 14. That looks like training overlap (or a timbre 6s can't handle), so I'd discount that set.
  - The non-MUSDB Cambridge excerpts (`cmtx`, +2.4) and the MUSDB test songs (`cmt8`, +3.2) agree in direction and roughly in size. They are the most trustworthy evidence, but they are not proof of no overlap.
- **Open sets use MUSDB test stems as backing.** htdemucs (4-stem) never trained on MUSDB test. htdemucs_6s trained on Meta's internal data. Mega's data is unknown. The guitar parts don't match the backing in key or tempo, so the open sets only rank models against each other.
- **Our remixes aren't the released mixes.** Unity sums with no EQ, compression or reverb are drier and more separable than a produced mix. Absolute scores will differ on commercial songs; the ranking is what carries over.
- **Short items.**
  - The excerpts are 18–39 s and the open items 30 s, so the context models see is shorter than a full song.
  - `cmt8` uses 90 s of each song.
  - Only 8 of the 12 MUSDB test songs have guitar multitracks. BKS isn't listed any more, and the other three have no guitar.
- **CPU rates are from one 30 s excerpt at 1 thread** on a shared box. Kim's 14.3 here vs 5.71 on the Mac in round 2 shows how much the CPU matters. Re-time in the target container before deciding the budget.
- **Licences.**
  - Mega 53 weights: MIT, by the trainer's statement in MSST #245.
  - Kim: MIT.
  - htdemucs_6s: no clear grant, likely CC BY-NC (round 1).
  - Cambridge-MT audio: educational use only; used internally, never redistributed.
  - Guitar-TECHS, GuitarSet, EGDB-PG: CC BY 4.0.
  - All recorded in [../datasets.md](../datasets.md).
